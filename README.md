## 🛡️ Core Features

- **Entity & Relationship Mapping**: Visualizes complex networks of entities, devices, locations, and IPs using advanced physics-based graph rendering.
- **Behavioral Profiling**: Leverages AI to detect anomalies, suspicious behavioral trends, and critical operational threats.
- **Dynamic Risk Scoring**: Real-time evaluation of entities to surface high-risk targets.
- **Community Clustering**: Automatically detects isolated cells and operational subgroups within a larger intelligence web.
